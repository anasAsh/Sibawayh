# Banking and finance register (الصياغة المصرفية والمالية)

Read this when writing a bank notice or letter, a loan or financing agreement, account terms, a card agreement, a statement note, or any text written as or to a bank, or about accounts, loans, transfers or financial products.

Markers: ✗ wrong or out of register, ✓ correct.

## 1. When it applies

| The text is | Register |
|---|---|
| A bank's letter, notice, account terms, loan/financing agreement, statement note, or fintech product copy about accounts, cards, loans or transfers | This file |
| A news summary that mentions a bank or an interest rate in passing | Plain MSA; not this file |

### 2.1 Interest and profit — pick the right term

Arabic banking has two separate vocabularies depending on whether the product is conventional or Islamic/sharia-compliant. Do not mix them:

| Conventional (interest-bearing) | Islamic/sharia-compliant (profit-based) |
|---|---|
| الفائدة (interest) | الربح / العائد (profit/return) |
| سعر الفائدة (interest rate) | نسبة الربح / معدل العائد |
| قرض بفائدة (interest-bearing loan) | تمويل بالمرابحة / الإجارة / المشاركة (contract-specific financing) |

If the user doesn't say which kind of institution or product this is, ask, or default to the conventional vocabulary (الفائدة) for a generic bank and keep it consistent through the whole text; never use الفائدة for a product explicitly described as Islamic or sharia-compliant.

## 3. Document shape

### 3.1 Addressing the customer

> عميلنا الكريم / السيد/ة ... المحترم/ة،

### 3.2 Notice and statement phrasing

- Informing: نحيطكم علمًا بأنّ... / نود إعلامكم بأنّ... / يُرجى العلم أنّ...
- Requesting action: يُرجى التكرّم بـ... / نرجو منكم...
- Stating an obligation on the customer: يلتزم العميل بسداد... في موعد لا يتجاوز...
- Figures: always digits for amounts, rates and dates, with the currency name or code right after the amount and the same form used throughout one document (✓ 500 دينار أردني، 500 د.أ. — pick one and keep it).

### 3.3 Closing

> وتفضّلوا بقبول فائق الاحترام،
> [اسم البنك / الموظف المسؤول]

## 4. Register notes

- Formal, precise, numeric: state the rate, period and amount exactly; avoid vague quantifiers (كثير من الأرباح) where a number belongs.
- Regulatory caution is part of this register: avoid absolute promises in marketing or product copy (✗ عائد مضمون 100%, ✗ بدون أي مخاطر) — state the condition instead (✓ عائد تقديري يعتمد على أداء الصندوق).
- Gloss an acronym on first use (✓ رقم الحساب المصرفي الدولي (آيبان))، then use the Arabic term or the established abbreviation alone.
- No dialect, no conversational filler; this register stays formal even in a short SMS-style notice.
