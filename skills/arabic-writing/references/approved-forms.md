# Approved forms: never "fix" these

Read this before flagging or changing any word that a purist "قل ولا تقل" list rejects, and whenever a checker finding or your own instinct says a common modern form is wrong.

Purist books (العدناني، مصطفى جواد، أسعد داغر) reject many forms that مجمع اللغة العربية بالقاهرة later approved through تضمين (a verb or preposition taking on another's meaning), قياس or attestation. Every form below is **correct**. Never flag it, never change it in someone else's text, never list it as an error. When you write from scratch you may choose the purist form where it reads more elegantly (e.g. ✓ في الوقت نفسه), but the approved form is never a mistake.

The checker skips these forms (`scripts/data/approved_forms.txt`; keep the two lists in sync).

## Contested forms: also correct, never mention

Purists reject the forms below, usage is overwhelming, and no Academy ruling was confirmed. Treat them as **correct**: do not flag them, do not "correct" them, do not mention them to the user, not even as a soft suggestion.

- ✓ طالما (meaning "as long as")
- ✓ بينما (mid-sentence, contrastive)
- ✓ سويًّا (meaning "together")
- ✓ كافة الطلاب، الكافة
- ✓ أمر هامّ
- ✓ تواجد، متواجد (meaning "be present")
- ✓ الرئيسي، الرئيسية
- ✓ استبيان
- ✓ العديد من
- ✓ مؤخرًا
- ✓ متأكد من
- ✓ استلم الرسالة
- ✓ مبروك
- ✓ شيّق
- ✓ الكثير من
- ✓ قراءة وكتابة الدرس (two مضاف sharing one مضاف إليه)
- ✓ مباني، معاني، أغاني as indefinite plurals (strict grammar: مبانٍ; the Academy allows keeping the ي)
- ✓ حيث أنّ (classical: حيث إنّ)

One exception for density only: قام بـ + masdar is in this purist group too, but a cluster of it is a translationese marker. Handle it under style.md's density principle, never as a single-instance error.
